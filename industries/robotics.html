Industries · Robotics

Navigation technologies for robots operating beyond reliable GNSS.

PhoQtek Labs develops visual, inertial, and sensor-fusion technologies for robotic and autonomous systems operating indoors, in industrial facilities, infrastructure environments, campuses, warehouses, and GNSS-limited areas.

For robotics, PhoQtek supports platform-specific navigation development rather than a universal product for all environments.

Relevant platforms

  • Indoor mobile robots
  • Warehouse robots
  • Industrial inspection robots
  • Infrastructure inspection robots
  • Defense robots
  • Autonomous patrol systems
  • Tunnel and underground robots
  • Campus and facility mobility platforms
