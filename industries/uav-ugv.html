Industries · UAV & UGV

Navigation resilience for unmanned platforms beyond GPS.

PhoQtek Labs develops GPS-denied navigation technologies for unmanned aerial and ground platforms operating where GNSS/GPS is jammed, spoofed, degraded, obstructed, or unavailable.

The objective is to reduce single-point dependence on GNSS through complementary onboard sensing under defined operating conditions.
