Industries · Industrial & Underground

GPS-denied navigation support for underground and industrial mobility.

PhoQtek Labs develops visual, inertial, and sensor-fusion technologies for platforms operating where GNSS/GPS is unavailable, obstructed, or unreliable — tunnels, mines, pipe-jacking, industrial inspection, and underground corridors.

FOG-based inertial sensing is relevant where heading support is needed without satellite visibility. Visual navigation may support localization in visually structured environments, subject to lighting, dust, and sensor placement.
