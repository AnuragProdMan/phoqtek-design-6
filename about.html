About PhoQtek Labs

Building indigenous navigation
technologies beyond reliable GNSS.

PhoQtek Labs is a deep-tech company developing GPS-denied navigation technologies for defense, aerospace, unmanned systems, robotics, autonomous platforms, and selected industrial mobility applications.

Mission

Reduce dependence on
external satellite signals.

To build indigenous navigation technologies for platforms operating beyond reliable GNSS. PhoQtek is focused on onboard sensing, optical inertial sensing, visual navigation, embedded electronics, and platform-specific integration.

Development philosophy

Not solved by one sensor.

Visual sensing is environment-dependent. Inertial sensing drifts over time. Platform dynamics, vibration, timing, SWaP, thermal exposure, and integration quality all affect real-world performance. PhoQtek follows staged engineering from physics-level validation to platform-specific demonstrators.

PhoQtek works with platform developers, defense users, aerospace teams, UAV/UGV manufacturers, robotics companies, system integrators, laboratories, and industrial partners. PhoQtek is part of the NVIDIA Inception Program.
