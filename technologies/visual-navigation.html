Visual Navigation
for GPS-denied autonomy.

PhoQtek’s Visual Navigation System is being developed to support autonomous and mission-relevant platforms operating where GNSS/GPS is unavailable, degraded, obstructed, jammed, spoofed, or unreliable.

It is intended to complement inertial sensors, platform control systems, mission computers, and other onboard navigation sources. Public pages do not disclose algorithm architecture or proprietary internals.
