FOG · under staged development

Optical inertial
sensing.

PhoQtek Labs is developing Fiber-Optic Gyroscope technology as part of its indigenous inertial navigation roadmap for platforms operating beyond reliable GNSS.

Heading holds
when cameras fail.

A FOG measures rotation using light in an optical fiber path. Rotation produces a measurable optical phase difference converted into angular-rate information.

Staged development.

FOG-based sensing can contribute angular-rate information to inertial navigation, stabilization, heading support, visual navigation, and sensor fusion. It is presented as under staged development.
