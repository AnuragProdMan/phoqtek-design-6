01 / The problem

Satellite navigation
cannot be assumed.

GNSS can be blocked or degraded indoors, underground, inside tunnels, near dense infrastructure, in urban canyons, or under foliage — and disrupted by interference, jamming, spoofing, or operational denial.

The objective is not universal GPS replacement, but platform-specific navigation technology for environments where GNSS is unavailable, degraded, or cannot be treated as the sole trusted source.

02 / Core technologies

Four development
streams.

PhoQtek approaches GPS-denied navigation as a system-level engineering challenge spanning photonics, inertial sensing, visual navigation, embedded systems, and integration.

Visual Navigation System

Onboard visual sensing and embedded processing to provide navigation-relevant information in GNSS-degraded environments.

Open page →

Fiber-Optic Gyroscope

Optical inertial sensing under development, contributing angular-rate information to the navigation stack.

Staged development →

Embedded Electronics

Onboard processing, sensor interfaces, data handling, and platform integration within power and thermal constraints.

Sensor-Fusion Engineering

Complementary sensing architectures combining visual, inertial, platform, and mission-relevant inputs under defined operating conditions.

03 / Industries

Platforms operating
beyond reliable GNSS.

01

Defense

Navigation resilience for platforms expected to operate in GNSS-degraded, GNSS-denied, and electronically contested environments.

 02

Aerospace

Navigation resilience and inertial backup for aerospace and autonomous flight systems in operationally complex environments.

 03

UAV & UGV

Reducing single-point dependence on GNSS for unmanned aerial and ground platforms through complementary onboard sensing.

 04

Robotics

Navigation technologies for robots operating indoors, in industrial facilities, infrastructure environments, and GNSS-limited areas.

 05

Autonomous Vehicles

Navigation-relevant sensing for autonomous mobility in urban canyons, tunnels, underpasses, and GNSS-obstructed corridors.

 06

Industrial & Underground

Tunnels, mines, pipe-jacking, trenchless construction support, indoor inspection, and underground corridor navigation.
Claim boundary

PhoQtek’s GPS-denied navigation technologies are under staged development and platform-specific validation. Performance depends on platform dynamics, environment, sensor configuration, mission duration, compute resources, mechanical integration, and operating conditions. Public performance claims are made only after controlled testing and application-specific evaluation.
